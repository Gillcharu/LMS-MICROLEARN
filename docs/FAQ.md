# MicroLearn FAQ

## Why microlearning?
Microlearning improves engagement with focused short sessions.

## Are quiz rules enforced?
Yes. Lessons must have 3-5 quiz questions.

## Can lessons be long?
No. Duration is restricted to 5-15 minutes.

## Do creators and learners have different access?
Yes. Creator Studio is role-protected.

## How is media managed?
The backend supports external cloud media URL references and a storage abstraction.
